===============================================================================
ELITE FORCE VR - CONTROLS
===============================================================================

Default layout: right handed, Swap Sticks off.
Your weapon is in your RIGHT hand.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MOVEMENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Left stick                Walk / strafe
  Right stick  (left/right) Turn
  Right stick  (pull down)  Crouch  (toggle - pull down again to stand)
  Left stick   (click)      Walk / run toggle
  A                         Jump

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WEAPONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Right trigger             Fire
  B                         Alt fire
  Right grip  (hold)        Weapon wheel - point at a weapon, release to take it

Both fire controls are on your weapon hand. The off hand trigger does nothing.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ITEMS AND INFORMATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Right stick (click)       Use  (doors, panels, consoles)
  Left grip   (hold)        Inventory wheel - tricorder, hypos, save, load
  X           (hold)        Mission objectives
  Y                         Open / close the game menu
  Menu button (either hand) Open / close the game menu


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CUTSCENES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A                         Skip the cutscene
