===============================================================================
ELITE FORCE VR - CONTROLS
===============================================================================

Default layout: right handed, Swap Sticks off.
Your weapon is in your RIGHT hand.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MOVEMENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Left stick                Walk / strafe
  Right stick  (left/right) Turn
  Right stick  (pull down)  Crouch  (toggle - pull down again to stand)
  Left stick   (click)      Walk / run toggle
  A                         Jump

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WEAPONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Right trigger             Fire
  B                         Alt fire
  Right grip  (hold)        Weapon wheel - point at a weapon, release to take it

Both fire controls are on your weapon hand. The off hand trigger does nothing.

TWO HANDED WEAPONS
  Put your left hand in front of your right hand, along the gun, and hold the
  left grip. The gun now points from your right hand to your left hand. This
  steadies the aim and cuts the spread of the Scavenger rifle and the Tetrion
  disruptor to one third. Let go of the grip to return to one hand.
  If your left hand is not in front of the gun, the left grip opens the
  inventory wheel as usual.

COMPRESSION RIFLE SCOPE
  Hold the compression rifle with both hands and bring it up to your face.
  The scope opens and zooms the view. You move at one third speed while you
  look through the scope. Push the right thumbstick up to zoom in and pull it
  down to zoom out. The small red cross in the centre shows where the shot
  hits. The HUD is hidden while the scope is open. Lower the rifle or let go
  of the left grip to close the scope.

CROSSHAIR
  The crosshair shows where your weapon points, on the surface it hits.
  Pick it in the Preferences menu (Crosshair). Pick "None" to turn it off.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ITEMS AND INFORMATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Right stick (click)       Use  (doors, panels, consoles)
  Left grip   (hold)        Inventory wheel - tricorder, hypos, save, load
  X           (hold)        Mission objectives
  Y                         Open / close the game menu
  Menu button (either hand) Open / close the game menu

IN MENUS
  Point a controller at the menu to move the cursor. Pull its trigger to
  click. Pull the trigger on the other hand to point with that hand instead.
  A also clicks. B, Y or the menu button goes back.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CUTSCENES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A                         Skip the cutscene
